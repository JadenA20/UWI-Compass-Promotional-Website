How Does UWI Compass Work?

Understandably, there may some questions about how UWI Compass operates. Below are a few tutorial videos to showcase the features of the system, and hopefully answer those questions.

User Login

Students are able to access UWI Compass using their Student ID and Password. Additionally, the UWI Compass Terms of Service must be accepted in order to fully complete the login process. Students will not be asked to accept these terms again once they have done so the first time.

Dashboard

Once logged in to UWI Compass, students are shown the following:

  • Degree Details: Major, Minor GPA and Progress
  • Academic Summary: Provides a list of completed courses, sorted by foundation, and course level
  • Skill Profile: Created once a student generates a recommendation of courses to take

Additionally, the student gains access to a sidebar, which allows quick navigation between the Dashboard, Courses, and Recommendations

Courses

From Courses, students can choose to browse by faculty, or use the search bar. The option to browse by department is also possible, once a faculty has been selected.

Reviews

Once a course has been selected, students are able to view more course details, such as the course description, and semester(s) offered. Alongside those are Reviews, which can only be left by students who have completed the course.

Forums

Forums are directly accessed from a selected course. All students can participate here, whether by creating their own post, or by replying to the posts of othe students.

Recommendations

From Recommendations, students can choose the semester and course level for which they want their recommendations. Before each generation is made, the student is reminded to complete their own research, and avoid using UWI Compass as a sole resource. For students who are yet to complete any courses, they may select from a variety of skills to recieve their course recommendations.
