Benefits of UWI Compass

Our system provides the following benefits to its users (students):

Decision-making

Helps to facilitate the decision-making process for students (in relation to choosing courses).

Supplementary Tool

Acts as a supplementary tool to official academic advising

Convenient

Provides students with a convenient aid for course planning during the semester at any time they wish to do some course planning.

High Success

Helps to drive the high success of students in their academic journeys by recommending courses that best match students based on their academic history and/or their preferred skills.

Foundation

Aids in creating a foundation for the course planning process for first year students who have no academic history, by providing them with an idea of how their future semester course selections can possibly be arranged based on their interested skills.

Course Insights & Discussions

The system helps students find courses of interest by providing descriptions and reviews from past students. It also facilitates discussions among peers through forums regarding these courses.

Peer Advice

This system provides a platform for students to receive advice from their peers regarding courses they are interested in taking via forums.
