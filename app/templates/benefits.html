Benefits

Why Use UWI Compass?

There are several benefits of using UWI Compass, as shown below.

Decision-making

Helps to facilitate the decision-making process for students during the course selection process.

Supplementary Tool

Acts as a supplementary tool to official academic advising

Convenient

Provides students with a convenient aid for course planning at any time they wish.

Improves Success

Helps to improve the success of students during their academic journey by recommending courses that best suit their academic history and/or preferred skills.

Foundation

Aids as a foundation for the course planning process for first year students who have no academic history, by providing them with an idea of how their future semester course selections could be arranged based on skills that interest them.

Course Insights & Discussions

Helps students find courses of interest by providing descriptions and reviews from past students. It also facilitates discussions among peers through forums regarding these courses.

Peer Advice

Provides a platform for students to receive advice from their peers regarding courses they are interested in (or actively taking) via forums.
